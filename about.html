Who We Are

Wind Load Solutions is a wind-engineering practice in Naples, Florida, founded in 2002. For more than two decades we have done one thing and done it well: calculating accurate wind loads on buildings and structures to the ASCE 7 standard. That single-minded focus is why our calculations have backed a 100% permit-approval record over 24 years — across single-family homes, rooftop and ground-mount solar, signs and billboards, specialty structures, and Risk Category IV essential facilities.

In that time the standard has advanced through seven editions of ASCE 7, and we have worked through every one. Today we calculate and explain wind loads to the latest edition, ASCE 7-22, and help you reconcile it with whichever edition your local building code has actually adopted — often an earlier one.

Standards, Codes, and the Latest Edition

ASCE 7 is the standard that defines how wind loads are calculated; the IBC, IRC, and state codes such as the Florida Building Code are the codes that adopt it. We always work to the latest ASCE 7 (currently ASCE 7-22) and help you reconcile it with the edition your jurisdiction has adopted — which is frequently an earlier one. We are careful never to overstate a software product: our calculators produce a permit-ready engineering report, while sealing remains a separate Professional Engineer service.
