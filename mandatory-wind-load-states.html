WHY THESE STATES

What drove the mandate

Hurricane Exposure

All 10 states face major hurricane or tropical-storm exposure; Florida has seen $328B in hurricane damage since 1900.

Insurance Pressure

Massive payouts after Andrew, Katrina, and Sandy pushed insurers to demand stricter codes and documentation.

Life Safety

Building failures in high-wind events have cost lives; mandatory calcs ensure structures survive design-level wind.

Property Protection

Code-compliant buildings suffer 40–60% less damage in hurricanes, protecting value and cutting repair costs.

Federal Incentives

FEMA's Community Rating System and BRIC grants incentivize states to adopt stricter building codes.

Economic Recovery

States with stronger codes recover faster after disasters — less damage means a quicker return to business.
