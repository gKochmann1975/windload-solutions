ASCE 7 · DESIGN METHODS · ASD ↔ LRFD

Convert wind loads between ASD and LRFD

Both methods read the same wind pressure from ASCE 7. The bridge between them is a single load factor: LRFD carries the full ultimate pressure (1.0W); ASD takes 0.6 of it.

×0.6LRFD → ASD PRESSURE
√0.6EQUIVALENT ASD SPEED ≈ 0.775 V
×1.667ASD → LRFD REVERSE (1 ÷ 0.6)

THE ONE FACTOR

One number bridges ultimate and service

The wind pressure equation never changes. Only the load factor applied to W moves you between strength-level (LRFD) and service-level (ASD).

TWO QUANTITIES, TWO RULES

Pressure scales by 0.6 — speed by √0.6

Pressure is proportional to V², so the speed factor is the square root of the pressure factor. Mixing them up is the most common error.

Pressure → × 0.6

ASD wind pressure = 0.6 × ultimate (LRFD) pressure. Reverse with × 1.667 (1 ÷ 0.6).

q_ASD = 0.6 · q_ULT

Speed → × √0.6

Equivalent ASD wind speed = V_ult × √0.6 ≈ 0.775 V_ult, because pressure ∝ V².

V_ASD ≈ 0.775 · V_ULT

ONE CONSISTENT EXAMPLE

From 150 mph ultimate to ASD

Exposure C, height 30 ft (K_z = 0.98), K_zt = 1.0, K_d = 0.85, using q = 0.00256 · K_z · K_zt · K_d · V².

150V_ULT (MPH)
≈116V_ASD = 150 × √0.6 (MPH)
≈48.0q_ULT (PSF)
≈28.8q_ASD = 0.6 × q_ULT (PSF)
StepComputationResult
Ultimate pressure (LRFD)0.00256 × 0.98 × 1.0 × 0.85 × 150²≈ 48.0 psf
ASD pressure (× 0.6)0.6 × 48.0≈ 28.8 psf
Equivalent ASD speed (× √0.6)150 × 0.775≈ 116 mph
Check — pressure from 116 mph0.00256 × 0.98 × 1.0 × 0.85 × 116²≈ 28.8 psf
Reverse: ASD → LRFD (× 1.667)28.8 × 1.667≈ 48.0 psf

The 28.8 psf computed two ways — once by × 0.6 and once from the √0.6 speed — match exactly. That is the internal consistency check: pressure ∝ V² guarantees (√0.6)² = 0.6.

AVOID THESE

Common conversion mistakes

Each of these silently over- or under-loads a member. Keep pressure and speed factors separate, and apply 0.6 to pressure only.

Scaling speed by 0.6

The 0.6 factor applies to pressure, not wind speed. Speed scales by √0.6 ≈ 0.775.

SPEED ≠ × 0.6

Re-deriving the pressure

You do not recompute q from scratch. The same ASCE 7 pressure is reused — only the factor on W changes.

SAME q

Inverting the wrong way

ASD → LRFD divides by 0.6 (× 1.667). Multiplying ASD by 0.6 again drives the load far too low.

÷ 0.6 = × 1.667

Mixing factor with K_d

The 0.6 ASD factor is independent of the directionality factor K_d = 0.85 already inside q. Do not double-count.

K_d STAYS IN q
