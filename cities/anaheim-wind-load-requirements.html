WIND CLIMATE · EXPOSURE

Inland, seismic-governed design

Anaheim sits roughly 10-15 miles inland behind dense suburban terrain, so wind speeds stay low and earthquake loads typically govern. Wind still must be checked for permits.

85-95 mph basic wind

Risk Category II, 3-second gust per the ASCE 7-22 maps for inland Orange County.

LOW-WIND CLIMATE

Exposure B (suburban)

Continuous development with buildings, trees and obstructions covers virtually all of Anaheim.

SECTION 26.7

Seismic dominates

Newport-Inglewood, Whittier and San Andreas systems make earthquake loads the lead design driver.
